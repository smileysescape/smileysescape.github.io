Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what information the Smileys escape app ("the app") for iPhone uses. The app is developed by Anthony Stepennov.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress — scores, coins, bonus points and settings such as sound and vibration — is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the app submits your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

The app offers optional rewarded video ads, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads. This information is processed by Google as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • Tracking permission. iOS asks whether you allow the app to track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If you are in these regions, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time with the Privacy button in the app's Settings screen.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
